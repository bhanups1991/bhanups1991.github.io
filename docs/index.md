### Welcome to My Research Website

Welcome to my digital working notebook and technical log. This space serves as an open extension of my physical research notebooks. Here, I document literature notes, derivations, and implementation principles focused on my work in **computational solid mechanics, finite element analysis, and contact mechanics**.

### Core Principle

I try to explain things as simply as I possibly can. I have observed that as I progress in research, understanding books and papers is getting increasingly difficult. The reason, I suspect, is that technical literature is often too terse—tightly worded, sharp, compressed, and clinical. More often than not, several key concepts and notations are assumed a priori. While this may suit some (perhaps a large community), I sometimes find it a bit difficult to follow and tend to fall asleep as my brain shuts down. Naturally, this is a personal observation, and others may view the same papers differently based on their own backgrounds. Ultimately, I am documenting these concepts, equations, and implementations for my own future reference and for anyone else who might find them useful. I warmly welcome any comments or feedback you may have to help improve the material shared here.
