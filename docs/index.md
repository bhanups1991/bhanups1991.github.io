### Welcome to My Research Website

Welcome to my digital working notebook and technical log. This space serves as an open extension of my physical research notebooks. Here, I document literature notes, derivations, and implementation principles focused on my work in **computational solid mechanics, finite element analysis, and contact mechanics**.

### Core Principle

In these notes, I try to explain things as simply as I possibly can. I have observed that technical literature is often too terse—tightly worded, sharp, compressed, and clinical. More often than not, several key concepts and notations are assumed a priori. While this may suit some (perhaps a large community), I sometimes find it a bit difficult to follow and tend to fall asleep as my brain shuts down. Naturally, this is a personal observation, and others may view the same research papers differently based on their own backgrounds. Ultimately, I am documenting these concepts, equations, and implementations for my own future reference and for anyone else who might find them useful. The reader should also keep in mind that there is no particular order in these notes. Moreover the level of difficulty varies from note to note. Some of these are revisions of old concepts from undergraduate to graduate level mechanics, while others are at the postgraduate research level. I warmly welcome any comments or feedback you may have to help improve the material shared here.
